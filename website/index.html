For tabletop game masters

The sound of your table, at your fingertips

Comilito is a free desktop soundboard and mixer for Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu and every other table. Build scenes from music, ambience and effects, then stack them live as the story turns.

Download View on GitHub

Three kinds of sound

M

Music

Background tracks, one at a time, with crossfades between them.

A

Ambience

Looping layers — rain, tavern chatter, wind — stacked as deep as you like.

E

Effects

One-shots on a pad: a door slam, a dragon's roar, a spell going off.

Built for running a session

The Tapestry

Stack scenes on top of each other. Higher scenes duck the ambience below them, and the top scene with music plays its music. A fight breaks out over the rainy night; when it ends, the rain is still there.

Hotkeys and presets

Space starts and pauses, Esc stops everything, and each scene can have its own key. Save a stack you reuse as a preset and bring it back in one move.

Videos for the players

Give a scene a video and it plays in a window of its own, ready for a second screen or a TV at the table.

Your files, your folders

Each campaign is a plain folder with its audio inside. Sync it, back it up, or export it as a single .comilito file to share with another game master.

Themes

Comilito comes with three themes — Codex, Ironwood and Birch — and lets you make your own. These extra themes are ready to download: in Comilito, open Preferences › Appearance › Import theme file… and pick the file.
